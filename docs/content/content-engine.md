# DoodleOS Content Engine

## Purpose

Turn DoodleOS into a repeatable content system, not just a static site.

## Content Pillars

1. Doodle diagnostics
2. Incident reports
3. Field Guide entries
4. Archetypes
5. Error codes
6. Share posts

## Rules

- Dry, operational, and specific
- DoodleOS first, DoodleOS 95 Mode second
- No generic dog-blog tone
- No excessive cuteness
- Every joke should feel like a system trying to explain chaos

## Weekly Output Target

- 5 error codes
- 3 share posts
- 2 field guide entries
- 1 archetype
- 1 incident report
