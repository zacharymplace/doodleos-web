# Pack 001 Production Readiness

## Pre-Print Readiness Boundary

Pack 001 is ready to enter physical validation when the source SVGs have been assembled into one 8.5 x 11 inch sheet and every item in the [Pre-Print checklist](./print-run-checklist.md#pre-print) has been confirmed against the final-size export.

Crossing this boundary means the digital artifact is prepared for a controlled test print. It does not mean the pack is validated for batch production, packaging, sale, or fulfillment.

## Artifact Preparation Completed

The repository contains the approved preparation inputs:

- Six Pack 001 SVG source assets
- The sheet format, layout, spacing, and cut-path guidance
- The print-run checklist for pre-print, test-print, packaging, and launch review

These materials define how to assemble and evaluate the printable artifact. Repository review can confirm file presence, structure, and documented constraints, but it cannot confirm printer output, stock behavior, color reproduction, legibility at physical size, or cut accuracy.

## Physical Validation Still Required

Physical readiness remains unvalidated until a real sheet is printed and inspected. The test must verify:

- Final-size text remains legible
- Muted colors remain distinguishable under normal indoor light
- Borders print cleanly and survive cutting
- Cut paths preserve safe margins and do not clip artwork
- The selected stock produces the intended finish and handling quality

Digital completion must not be represented as production approval. Any failed physical check returns the artifact to preparation for adjustment and another controlled test.

## Controlled Handoff

The next authorized production step is exactly one controlled test-sheet print using the final-size sheet export and intended print-and-cut settings.

Record the printer, stock, export settings, cut settings, and observed results while completing the [Test Print checklist](./print-run-checklist.md#test-print). Do not begin batch production until that single sheet passes every test-print check and any resulting artifact changes have been reviewed in the pre-print checklist again.
